1. Local-First, On-Device Data Isolation

Zero cloud middlemen · Direct hardware writes

Every guest check-in, room folio, payment transaction, and passport scan is stored in a local, encrypted SQLite database resident on your physical mobile device. LogStay operates completely disconnected from any external cloud database.

Because there is no central server harvesting your property's records:

  • No guest identity details are ever sold, traded, or indexed by third-party data brokers.
  • No behavioral profiling, targeted advertising trackers, or telemetry SDKs exist in the binary.
  • Your revenue numbers, guest lists, and occupancy trends belong 100% to you.

2. Automated 02:00 AM ID Photo Shredder

DPDP Act Section 8(7) Automated Data Erasure

Under Section 8(7) of the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, data fiduciaries must erase personal data as soon as the specified purpose is fulfilled. Storing guest passport photos indefinitely on unsecured staff phones is a massive civil liability.

LogStay solves this through an autonomous background cleanup engine:

Scheduled Retention Enforcement Protocol:

1. Photos are marked with a statutory TTL (Time-To-Live) at check-in.

2. At 02:00 AM local property time, expired photos are permanently unlinked and overwritten with random bytes.

3. An immutable purge confirmation is appended to the local audit ledger: [PURGE_SUCCESS] Expired identity binaries erased. Zero residual liability.

3. Statutory Form C Foreign Guest Reporting

Immigration bureau compliance without cloud exposure

By statutory tourism mandates, hospitality properties accommodating international tourists must register foreign nationals with immigration authorities (Form C). Traditional PMS providers transmit unencrypted passport PDFs to foreign cloud servers to parse this data.

LogStay uses an on-device ICAO 9303 MRZ scanner. The Machine Readable Zone is decoded in 120ms directly on the phone's CPU. The Form C report is generated locally and exported directly by the authorized property administrator, eliminating unvetted third-party intermediaries.

4. Dual-Key Cash Audits & Anti-Tamper Ledgers

Cryptographic proof for shift handovers

Cash theft and unrecorded pocketing are prevented through mathematical proofs:

  • Both outgoing and incoming front desk staff must enter their independent verification PINs to close a shift.
  • Cash drawer counts are cross-checked against opening float plus atomic transaction receipts.
  • Any variance halts the handover, prompts for mandatory clerk justification, and sends a push notification to the owner's device.
